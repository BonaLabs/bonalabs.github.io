2. Gludin equipment / sword chamber
Source page: https://lineage2.plaync.com/conts/2026/261007_update#equipment
Original asset: https://assets.playnccdn.com/res/lineage2/update/2026/261007_update/2nd/pc/v2.0.0/img/second/equipment-background.webp
Usage: default featured article cards, article covers, and tracker cover.
Local delivery copies:
- gludin-equipment.jpg: 1920 x 1080
- gludin-equipment-960.jpg: 960 x 540

The equipment wallpaper shows a suspended sword above a pedestal inside a
vaulted chamber, lit by a central shaft of light. Campaign text/UI is excluded.
Only JPEG encoding and proportional resizing were applied to source artwork.
Layout cropping, fade masks, and readability overlays are controlled by CSS.
Images are served locally; no publisher CDN assets are hotlinked by the site.
The earlier generated warrior illustration remains only in Codex's original
generation archive and is not used by the current site.

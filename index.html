    About BonaLabs

    It started with a simple problem: keeping track.

    Important changes can be spread across patch notes, translations, and follow-up corrections. BonaLabs started as my own place to keep them organized and easy to check later.

    Now those notes are shared with other players as clear articles, using consistent in-game terminology and enough context to see why a change matters. The tools come from the same idea: if a mechanic comes down to chances, costs, or too much guesswork, there should be a quicker way to work it out.

    BonaLabs is a fan-made project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the game’s developer or publisher.

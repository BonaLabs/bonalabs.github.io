About BonaLabs

It started with a simple problem: keeping track.

Important changes can be spread across patch notes, translations, and follow-up corrections. BonaLabs started as my own place to keep them organized and easy to check later.

Now those notes are shared with other players as clear references, using consistent in-game terminology and enough context to see why a change matters. The tools come from the same idea: if a mechanic comes down to chances, costs, or too much guesswork, there should be a quicker way to work it out.

BonaLabs is a fan-made project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the game’s developer or publisher.

The next step

More updates covered. More tools to use.

Everything on BonaLabs will stay free to access. When support options become available, contributions will help cover hosting and give me more time to research updates, keep existing references accurate, and work on new tools.

Common

Updates shared by BASIC and Essence.

Classes

  • The new Rare buffs Mystic Expert, Arcana Expert and Phantom Expert can be learned by their respective classes from Lv. 76 using one Rare Spellbook.
  • Enchanting Relic to +3 no longer changes Mystic Explosion into Mystic Explosion: Relic. Instead, using a magic skill has a 50% chance to deal an additional hit.

2. Blaze, Aqua Swirl and Twister, learned from the Wizard, Elven Wizard and Dark Wizard stages, have been replaced by Magic Spike.

3. Arcana Burst has been added to Arcana Lord.

  • It can be learned from the Warlock stage after completing the Lv. 40 class transfer.
  • It requires a two-handed magic blunt weapon.
  • Learning it removes Magic Spike, Fireball, Ice Bolt and Wind Strike.

4. Phantom Strike has been added to Spectral Master.

  • It can be learned from the Phantom Summoner stage after completing the Lv. 40 class transfer.
  • It requires a two-handed magic blunt weapon.
  • Learning it removes Magic Spike, Fireball, Ice Bolt and Wind Strike.

5. Soultaker's Death Spike maximum skill level has been increased to Lv. 45, and its icon and visual effects have changed. Learning levels have been adjusted; already learned skill levels remain unchanged.

6. Enhanced passive skills have been added to Wizard-type classes. Each can be learned from Lv. 76 using one Enhanced Spellbook.

ClassEnhanced Passive — EnchantableEnhanced Passive
ArchmageMana StoneFlame Mastery
Storm ScreamerMana FlowStorm Mastery
SoultakerVoid DrawVoid Mastery
  • With Mana Stone, Mana Flow or Void Draw enchanted to +3, using a magic skill has a 50% chance to trigger an additional-hit skill against the target.
  • Flame Mastery, Storm Mastery and Void Mastery only take effect while wearing robes.

7. Passive skills have been added to Arcana Lord and Spectral Master.

ClassEnhanced PassiveRare Passive
Arcana LordArcana MasteryArcana Harmony
Spectral MasterPhantom MasteryPhantom Resonance
  • The Enhanced passives require an Enhanced Spellbook, and the Rare passives require a Rare Spellbook. They can be learned from Lv. 76.
  • Arcana Mastery and Phantom Mastery can be enchanted.
  • Arcana Harmony and Phantom Resonance increase certain combat stats and the maximum M. Skill Critical Rate.

8. Magic Booster, a buff that improves Casting Speed before the third class transfer, has been added.

  • It replaces Concentration and consumes two Spirit Ore when used.
  • It can be learned from Lv. 20.
  • Available class lines: Archmage, Mystic Muse, Storm Screamer, Soultaker, Arcana Lord, Elemental Master, Spectral Master, Hierophant, Cardinal, Eva's Saint and Shillien Saint.
  • Learning Elemental Concentration as an Elemental Master does not remove Magic Booster.

9. Soultaker's Dark Burst maximum target count has been adjusted to five, and its visual effects have changed.

10. Archmage's Fire Vortex and Blazing Skin visual effects have changed.

11. Mystic Muse's Ice Vortex, Freezing Skin and Aqua Splash visual effects have changed.

12. Mystic Muse's Mystic Explosion icon has changed.

13. Storm Screamer's Wind Vortex and Tempest visual effects have changed.

14. Soultaker's Dark Vortex visual effects have changed.

15. Elemental Master's Elemental Resonance and Elemental Mastery icons have changed.

16. Applying an appearance to a Sacred Templar now gives it the unique male High Elf appearance. The statement that appearance changes are unavailable has been removed from the Lord Knight skill description.

17. Weight limits for certain classes have been increased.

Shared fixes and improvements

  1. Fixed an issue where EXP earned on World Servers was not counted in the Weekly EXP Acquisition Ranking.
  2. Fixed an issue where the Class No. 1 emblem was intermittently not applied to the top-ranked character of certain classes.
  3. Fixed an issue where items sometimes could not be registered in World Trade.
  4. Fixed misaligned text in the memo field of the Contacts window.
  5. Fixed an issue where enchant effects for B-grade and lower weapons displayed differently only for the Crow class.
  6. Del Lizardman Rogue monsters in Eva's Underwater Garden no longer attack first.

BASIC

Updates specific to BASIC.

Hunting zones

1. The number of monsters in the following hunting zones has been reduced:

  • National Cemetery
  • Fields of Massacre
  • Plains of Glory
  • Plains of Fierce Battle
  • Silent Valley

2. Adena rewards from monsters in Silent Valley and Plains of the Lizardmen have been adjusted.

Special hunting zone — Tower of Infinity

Magic skills obtained in the Tower of Infinity (무한의 탑) can no longer be manually deleted.

Special hunting zone — Orbis Hall

  1. Boss HP on the lower and upper levels has been increased, and a damage cap has been added.
  2. Lower- and upper-level bosses no longer spawn at the same location.
  3. Boss rewards on both levels have changed: one Dragon's Belt Box can be obtained when their HP reaches 66%, again at 33%, and upon defeating them.

New hunting zone — Timak Outpost

Timak Outpost has been added as a hunting zone, with a new destination in the Teleport menu.

Monster invasions

Attack and defense capabilities have been reduced for monsters in the Monster Invasion and Dark Legion Invasion encounters in Plains of the Lizardmen and Western Dragon Valley.

Boss Subjugation Battle

The attack and defense capabilities of the intermediate bosses Kesma (케스마), Plas (플라스), Vira (뷔라), Hiter (히테르) and Hirak (히라크) have been reduced.

Other changes

  1. Fixed an issue where Barkas (바르카스) and Crow could not progress the quests “Unleash the Power of Barkas” (바르카스의 힘을 해방하라) and “A Step Toward the Strongest” (최강을 향한 한 걸음) after their first class transfer.
  2. Certain monsters in Orbis Hall no longer use Stun skills.
  3. Tower Watcher monsters on Floor 1 of the Tower of Insolence no longer use Sleep or HP Drain skills.

Essence

Updates specific to Essence.

Raid rewards

Bartz-specific Spellbooks have been added to raid rewards.

Outpost Conquest

Five Black Coins (칠흑의 주화) have been added to the rewards for the “Defeat Outpost Monsters” mission in Outpost Conquest (전초기지 점령전).

Boss Subjugation Battle

The intermediate bosses Kesma (케스마), Plas (플라스), Vira (뷔라), Hiter (히테르) and Hirak (히라크) no longer award EXP.

Instance zone — Lindraco Canyon

  1. The area covered by tornadoes generated by Storm Seeds has been reduced.
  2. P. Atk. and M. Atk. have been reduced for all monsters in Lindraco Canyon, including area bosses.

L2 Store — Main Servers

The following permanent product has been added to the Ancient Adena Shop:

ProductAvailabilityCostPurchase Limit
Enhanced Spellbook (Sealed)Permanent10,000 Ancient AdenaOnce per account per month

Other changes

  1. Fixed an issue where the expired daily mission “Cyrax Temple Subjugation” was still displayed in the mission list.
  2. Fixed an issue where the hostile effects of certain skills affected characters from the same server group in the Black Citadel (칠흑의 성채).
  3. Fixed an issue where Bartz-class Spellbooks could not be selected from certain Heroic and Legendary Spellbook Exchange Coupons.
  4. Fixed a missing percentage symbol for Debuff and Control Resistance in the Abyssal Defense special-effect tooltip of Boots of the Deep Sea.

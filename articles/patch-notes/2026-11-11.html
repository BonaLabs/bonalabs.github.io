Common

Updates shared by BASIC and Essence.

Classes

1. A new skill, Swift Tempo, has been added to Warrior- and Knight-type classes of the Human, Elf, Dark Elf, Orc and Dwarf races.

  • Learning Swift Tempo reduces P. Skill Cooldown by 2%.
  • It can be learned from Lv. 40 by consuming 5 Magic Tablets.

  • The range of Power Strike, Fatal Strike, Wild Sweep, Iron Punch, Burning Fist (skill Lv. 1–45) and Guard Crush has been increased to 100.

3. Fixed an issue where Vanguard could learn Sain Thrust (사인 스러스트) without consuming SP. Learning the skill now costs 5,900 SP.

4. The visual effects of Inferno, Blizzard, Demon Wind and Rush have been changed.

5. The icons for Full Swing and Gigantic Swing have been changed.

Shared fixes and improvements

  1. World Trade now displays the Adena market price (lowest price) at the top.
  2. Fixed an issue where the “Insert Soul Crystal” option appeared in shield item menus.
  3. The MP Drain skill used by Shackle and Royal Cave Servant monsters in Dragon Valley has been changed to HP Drain.
  4. Fixed abnormal monster spawn locations in National Cemetery.
  5. Removed the mechanic that caused raid bosses to go berserk after a certain time in combat.
  6. Sel Mahum Assault Soldier monsters in Sel Mahum Barracks no longer use Stun skills.
  7. Fixed incorrect sound playback for Transcendent Lethal Shot.
  8. Fixed an issue where the Elemental Unicorn would not attack when Elemental Master used Elemental Bolt while Elemental Expert was active.

BASIC

Updates specific to BASIC.

Hunting zones

1. The number of monsters placed in the following hunting zones has been reduced:

  • National Cemetery
  • Fields of Massacre
  • Plains of Glory
  • Plains of Fierce Battle
  • Silent Valley

Seasonal hunting zone — Ant Nest

1. The seasonal Ant Nest hunting zone opens after scheduled maintenance on 11.11.2026 and remains available until scheduled maintenance begins on 09.12.2026.

  • Use Teleport (Alt+F) to travel to Ant Nest.
  • Only manual hunting is available; auto-hunting cannot be used.
  • A Cocoon Imbued with Ant Power NPC appears at a random location inside Ant Nest. Attacking it within 10 seconds gives a chance to spawn an Ancient Drone Ant.
  • Defeating monsters in Ant Nest restores MP.
  • A party bonus applies when defeating monsters while in a party.

Hunting zone — Tower of Insolence

1. Monster rewards in the Tower of Insolence have been adjusted. Certain monsters on Floor 5 and above can now drop Aden's Soul Crystal Lv. 1 — Weapon and Aden's Soul Crystal Lv. 1 — Armor.

2. The frequency at which bosses and elite monsters use skills has been adjusted.

Special hunting zone — Orbis Hall

1. Boss respawn times in the lower and upper levels are now randomly determined within a 10–30 minute interval.

2. Boss social aggro range has been increased, and their leash distance has been reduced.

Field raids

1. Ten field raids have been added to the Gludio and Giran territories.

Raid — Seven Star Apostles

1. The stats of Seven Star Apostles (칠성 사도) raid bosses have been reduced.

2. If a boss is not defeated within 10 minutes of the raid starting, its HP now recovers.

Special Crafting

1. The daily crafting limit for Magic Tablets has been increased from three to five times per account.

Other changes

  1. The Dye and Guardian categories are no longer displayed in World Trade.
  2. Hero's Escort Soldier and Hero's Royal Guard monsters in Orbis Hall no longer use Petrification skills.
  3. Fixed abnormal monster spawn locations in Timak Outpost.
  4. Sel Mahum Knight monsters no longer use Stun skills.

Essence

Updates specific to Essence.

Other changes

  1. Fixed an issue where characters could enter the interior of the Totem of Challenge in Turek Orc Camp.
  2. Fixed an issue where monsters in some parts of Hot Springs spawned on rocks and could not be attacked.
  3. Bartz's Heroic and Legendary Spellbooks have been added to the Tempest raid rewards.

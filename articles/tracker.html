    About this tracker

    The Korean official server is updated every Wednesday. This page translates the smaller changes that are useful to know before they reach the other regions but do not require a standalone article. Larger subjects continue to receive their own dedicated coverage.

    Entries are ordered by Korean application date, newest first. This cycle began on 04.06.2026 and remains active until its updates are rolled out globally.

    September 2026

    02.09.2026

    [Growth Bonus]

    1. The Sayha's Grace system has been removed, and its existing XP +300% effect has been converted into a permanent Growth Bonus.

    2. Since maintaining Sayha's Grace is no longer necessary, the Grace Maintenance server buff has been removed.

    3. Some buff items related to Sayha's Grace have been changed to increase XP/SP acquisition.

    4. Sayha's Grace-related items that no longer have a use have been converted into Spirit Ore and sent to the Personal Warehouse. Representative items include Grace Cookie (Sealed) and Sayha's Grace Scroll (Sealed).

    5. Sayha's Blessing and Sayha's Blessing (Sealed) will be deleted during the scheduled maintenance on 30.12.2026. Until then, using them allows them to be exchanged for one of the following:

    Exchange ItemExchange QuantityRequired Blessings
    Spirit Ore (Sealed)301
    Doll Summon Coupon (Common–Heroic) - 1-time (Sealed)1100
    Doll Summon Coupon (Common–Heroic) - 11-time (Sealed)11,000

    [Settings]

    1. A Very Low option has been added to the Effect Quality setting.

    • Compared with Low, this setting reduces decorative particles and additional visual effects for more optimized hunting and combat.

    2. A Minimize Other Character Effects option has been added.

    • Other characters' effects can be minimized for smoother gameplay during battles involving many characters.

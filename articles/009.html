Area Progression

The Instance Zone contains five areas, including the entry area. Completing each area unlocks the next.

  1. Speak with Sanctuary Guardian Kekropus to begin.
  2. Fill the gauge by defeating Heretic Soldiers and Heretic Guards. The gauge decreases when Maguen dies.
  3. Fill the gauge by defeating Heretic Soldiers, Heretic Guards, and Heretic Priests. Defeating Heretic Priests faster increases the gauge by a greater amount.
  4. Defeat Heretic Cassiel, Heretic Rabiel, Heretic Zaphiel, and Heretic Uriel within 3 seconds of one another. Otherwise, previously defeated monsters revive.
  5. Defeat Valian, Lord of the Collapsed Sanctuary. Valian summons subordinates twice, each targeting the party member identified by its title. If another character attacks that subordinate, it has a chance to recover all its HP.

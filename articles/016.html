Class overview

The new class 'Bartz' has been added.

AttributeDetails
Race High Elf
Gender Male
Combat Type Melee/Physical
Role Rogue
Equipment Dagger
  • The 'Bartz' class will be available for character creation from 20:00 KST on 28.10.2026.
  • When Bartz equips a Sigil, he reconstructs it with the power of light and uses it as a 'Radiant Dagger'.
  • Bartz cannot equip Shields.
  • Bartz receives a unique appearance based on the grade of the Upper Body armor equipped.
  • Bartz has 4 hairstyles, 3 facial appearances, and 3 hair color options.

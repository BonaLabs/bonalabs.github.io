Laboratory Lobby

A lobby screen has been added to Aden's Laboratory. From the lobby, you can see which bosses are currently available, select a boss, and begin its research.

  • The Acquired Effects window at the top of the lobby shows every effect currently applied to your character through ongoing research.
  • The Acquired Effects window inside an individual boss research screen shows only the effects obtained from that boss.

Frintezza Research

Frintezza has been added as a new Aden's Laboratory research boss. Activating the research costs Aden's Essence x2.

During common stages, selecting a card consumes Adena and Aden's Exploration Reports. The required amount depends on the server group and current stage.

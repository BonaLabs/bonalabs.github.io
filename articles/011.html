Armor Ensoul

Hardin's Soul Crystal and Hardin's Transcendent Soul Crystal can now be ensouled into A-grade or higher armor.

  • Sigils, Shields, and Sealed Equipment cannot be ensouled.
  • The Defense Property is applied separately according to the Helmet, Upper Body, Lower Body, Full Body, Gloves, or Boots slot.
  • Combat Power increases when Hardin's Soul Crystal Lv. 16–20 or Hardin's Transcendent Soul Crystal Lv. 1–5 is applied.

[Phoenix Knight]
1. Phoenix Shield
- The maximum number of affected targets has been changed to 20.
- Barrier HP has been changed to ‘7500 + 30% of the caster's Max HP’.

2. The new Legendary skill ‘Force of Phoenix: Master’ has been added.
Force of Phoenix: Master (Passive)
Basic Effects
Increases Max HP
Increases PvE Damage Bonus
Increases Bow/Dagger Resistance
Increases Speed
Activates ‘Phoenix Reverse’ on dying from attack

Phoenix Reverse
Restores 100% HP and applies a barrier and Imprisonment for 3 seconds
Awakens after 3 seconds, completing recovery
Trigger Cooldown: 600 sec.

Special Ability
Phoenix Shield → Phoenix Shield: Master
Enhanced Barrier HP
Added ally recovery effect

Phoenix Shield: Master
Applies ‘Phoenix Shield: Master’ and ‘Phoenix Damage Limit’ buffs to up to 20 nearby allies

Phoenix Shield: Master
Increases and heals allies' Max HP
Applies a barrier equal to 10000 + 35% of the caster's Max HP
The effect is removed when moving too far away from the caster
Duration: 20 sec.

Phoenix Damage Limit
Received Damage Limit: 5000
Duration: 5 sec.

Spirit Ore Consumption: 60

3. The new Rare recovery skill ‘Phoenix Recovery’ has been added.
- When acquired, removes the ‘Sacrifice’ skill.
Phoenix Recovery
Consumes 10% of current HP to heal up to 6 nearby allies and increase their P. Def.

Heals allies for 20% of the caster's Max HP
Increases allies' P. Def. by an amount equal to the caster's Shield Defense
The effect is removed when moving too far away from the caster

Cooldown: 180 sec.
Duration: 10 sec.
Spirit Ore Consumption: 5

- MP consumption has been removed and HP Cost: 3000 has been added.

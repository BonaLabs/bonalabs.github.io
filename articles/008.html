About this tracker

The Korean official server is updated every Wednesday. This page translates the smaller changes that are useful to know before they reach the other regions but do not require a standalone article. Larger subjects continue to receive their own dedicated coverage.

Entries are ordered by Korean application date, newest first. This cycle began on 04.06.2026 and remains active until its updates are rolled out globally.

1. New entries have been added to Combat Power Details.

Entry Description
P. Atk. from Atk. Spd. exceeding 2000 The rate at which Atk. Spd. exceeding 2000 is converted into P. Atk.
M. Atk. from Casting Spd. exceeding 2000 The rate at which Casting Spd. exceeding 2000 is converted into M. Atk.

M. Atk. gained from Casting Spd. exceeding 2000 The amount of M. Atk. gained from Casting Spd. exceeding 2000.
  • When Atk. Spd./Casting Spd. exceeds 2000 and the P. Atk./M. Atk. from Atk. Spd./Casting Spd. exceeding 2000 effect is applied, P. Atk./M. Atk. increases according to the effect's value.
  • Currently, this effect is available from a character skill (Totem Spirit Rabbit Lv. 2), Tempest Doll (Heroic grade or higher), and Aden Laboratory (Stage 3 Transcendence Effect).

2. Related functionality has been added to Combat Stats under Character Information.

  • For characters with the P. Atk./M. Atk. from Atk. Spd./Casting Spd. exceeding 2000 effect active, the color of Atk. Spd./Casting Spd. changes, and the amount of P. Atk./M. Atk. gained from the effect is displayed separately in parentheses.

Outpost Occupation Battle

'Outpost Occupation Battle' has been added.

Matching Dungeon Outpost Occupation Battle
Entry Level Lv. 85
Schedule Matching starts daily at 21:00
Participants 15 per server, up to 30 players
Features No XP loss upon PvP/PvE death
Players from the same server are allies, while players from other servers are neutral
Clan hostility and Revenge do not apply
Notes Automatic entry is unavailable while in combat

Since automatic entry is unavailable while in combat, it is recommended to wait in a safe location without being in combat when entry time approaches.

Matchmaking rewards

If matching fails, an alert icon appears at the bottom right, and rewards can be obtained through the icon.

  • If matching fails, an 'Outpost Occupation Battle Supply Box (Sealed)' is granted, along with priority matching for the Outpost Occupation Battle.
  • Matchmaking failure rewards can only be obtained before the next matching period begins.
  • Characters with matching priority will be matched first for the next Outpost Occupation Battle.
  • All matching priority is removed at the following day's matching time.
  • Using an 'Outpost Occupation Battle Supply Box (Sealed)' grants the items below.
ItemQuantity
Guaranteed Reward
Outpost Occupation Battle Defeat Reward Box (Sealed) x1
Pitch-Black Coin (Sealed) x10

Outposts and occupation

Each server is assigned to a Legion, and allies and enemies can be identified by the Legion mark displayed above each character.

  • The 'Outpost' and 'Central Base' can be targeted and occupied by using the 'Base Occupation' skill.
  • Occupation Score can be earned while maintaining control of an 'Outpost' or 'Central Base'.
  • Allied 'Outposts' lose Occupation Level over time, and the benefits provided according to Occupation Level are divided as follows.
Occupation Stage Buff Occupation Score
3 Outpost Protection Lv. 2 100 points per minute
2 Outpost Protection Lv. 1 50 points per minute
1 None None
  • Allied 'Outposts' at Occupation Stage 1 or lower can be targeted and restored by using the 'Base Repair' skill.
  • After occupying an Outpost, an 'Armored Supply Unit' that travels from the Legion Headquarters to that Outpost appears periodically.
  • If the Armored Supply Unit reaches the Outpost and successfully delivers supplies, its Occupation Level is fully restored.

Central Base and battle timeline

5 minutes after the match begins, the 'Four Heavenly Kings' appear in front of the Central Base gate.

  • Defeating the 'Four Heavenly Kings' that appear in front of the Central Base gate grants Occupation Score and opens the gate.
  • 10 minutes after the match begins, all Central Base gates open.
  • When the Central Base is occupied for the first time, the 'Executor' appears.
  • Defeating the 'Executor' grants Occupation Score.
  • The Legion that successfully occupies the Central Base earns 200 Occupation Score every minute, and a teleporter appears at the headquarters of the Legion that failed to occupy it.
  • The teleporter can be used to travel to 4 intersections.
  • The 'Pitch-Black Knights' appear periodically and attempt to neutralize the Central Base.
  • If the 'Pitch-Black Knights' successfully neutralize the Central Base, the teleporter despawns.
  • 15 minutes after the match begins, all monsters despawn and the Occupation Battle ends.

Battle rewards

After the match ends, rankings are determined by comparing each server's Occupation Score, and participants receive Server Battle Points and item rewards according to their server's ranking.

  • The character who deals the final blow to the 'Four Heavenly Kings' obtains 50 'Red Lantern (Time-limited) (Sealed)'.
  • The character who deals the final blow to the 'Executor' obtains 50 'Red Lantern (Time-limited) (Sealed)'.
